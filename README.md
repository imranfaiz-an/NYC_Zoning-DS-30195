For this project I studied the zoning and land use policy of NYC, which was the first in the country to implement a zoning policy in 1916 and hence has played a major role in outlining how each land parcel in the city can be used. After the zoning resolution of the 1916 the city passed another resolution in 1961, which was more elaborate and divided the city up into three main districts, namely Residential, Commercial and Manufacturing. 

Zoning amendments are still quite frequent with over 50 new changes being adopted in 2025 alone. For the project I attempted to spatially join a zoning amendment area with 311 complaints data to see how the complaints differ pre and post change. I specifically looked at the re-zoning case for South Jamaica in Queens which was one of the largest rezonings by area ever adopted.

All the code is in the Jupyter Notebook titled ```micro_project_zoning.ipynb``` and the data I used was the [NYC Zoning Features data](https://www.nyc.gov/content/planning/pages/resources/datasets/gis-zoning-features) and the 311 Complaints data from 2010-2012.
